# beSirius

> AI for the critical minerals supply chain. Every audit, certification, KYC and risk assessment your buyers, auditors and regulators require, automated across your entire value chain.

beSirius builds a Twin of every supplier — a continuously verified digital profile that maps each company's data to any framework, questionnaire, or regulation (CSDDD, CMRT, EMRT, AMRT, EcoVadis, CDP, S&P CSA, UNGC, LME Passport, CMSI, and more). The platform automates supplier due diligence, RMI/conflict-minerals reporting, and ratings improvement for the critical minerals value chain.
